Mobile app for photographers and their clients

Oh Shoot is an app for managing photoshoot days. It keeps everything a photographer needs in one place, helps align expectations with the client, and gets everyone to the shoot fully prepared. Gear lists, visuals and inspiration, a clear schedule for the day, and no misunderstandings or missed details along the way.

Digital store

A digital store focused on tactile product presentation, glass and frosted-bottle effects, and a smooth shopping flow across desktop and mobile.

The details

The starting point for this project was an existing brand: Spicehaus. The goal was to find where micro-animations could add character and personality to the shopping experience, without tipping into anything overdone or distracting. Simplicity and ease of use stayed the priority throughout, with the animation work layered on top rather than driving the design.

The Final Dinner

at Black's Manor
RSVP landing page & event app

An RSVP for a mysterious, highly exclusive dinner. Registrants aren't quite told what they're signing up for, only that it's very exclusive, and that if chosen, they get to play a meaningful character in the town's own story. The concept borrows the format of a murder mystery party, with the interaction style inspired by the board game Cluedo.

Kids Against The Machine

by Noga Erez
Vinyl design

Handmade vinyl packaging built around Noga Erez's fully acoustic album, Kids Against the Machine.

The concept

This is the acoustic version of Kids. I went with handwritten type and an anarchistic, raw vibe, since the album is about rebelling against machines, technology, and effects. Everything on it was recorded practically, so the design follows that same spirit.
